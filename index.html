I am a theoretical physicist working on gravity and quantum field theory beyond the usual relativistic setting. My research covers non-Lorentzian (Galilean and Carrollian) gravity and geometry, Carrollian quantum field theories, non-Lorentzian supergravity and string theory, and their connections to holography and black holes.

Research

Non-Lorentzian gravity and geometry

Newton–Cartan and Carrollian geometry, large-c and small-c expansions of general relativity, and three-dimensional gravity.

Carrollian quantum field theory

Quantization, symmetries, interactions and supersymmetry of Carrollian scalar and fermionic theories, and the electric and magnetic sectors.

Non-Lorentzian supergravity and strings

Galilean and Carrollian limits of supergravity and string theory, non-relativistic string worldsheets, and relations between different limits.

String dualities and Matrix theory

T-duality, SL(2,ℤ) duality and discrete light-cone quantization connecting non-Lorentzian string theories, including links to Matrix theory.

Flat-space holography

Carrollian geometry, BMS symmetries, boundary dynamics and holographic correlators in three-dimensional gravity.

Lower-dimensional gravity and black holes

Jackiw–Teitelboim (super)gravity, massive and multimetric gravity in three dimensions, and black hole information.

Open Positions

My TÜBİTAK 1001 project “Non-Lorentzian String Theory and Supergravity” (No. 125F467) at Istanbul University-Cerrahpaşa is looking for one postdoctoral researcher and one graduate student (M.Sc. or Ph.D.). The project studies non-Lorentzian string theory, supergravity and quantum field theory, with a focus on Newton–Cartan and Carrollian structures and their relation to Matrix theory, DLCQ and BFSS.

Postdoctoral researcher

Ph.D. in physics or a closely related field, with research experience in theoretical high-energy physics or related areas. Earlier work on non-Lorentzian theories is not required. Experience with symbolic/numerical computation, and the effective and critical use of AI-assisted tools in scientific research, is considered an advantage.

Send: CV, publication list, a short statement describing your research experience and interests, and contact details of referees.

Graduate student (M.Sc. / Ph.D.)

A background in physics or a related field and a strong interest in string theory, supergravity, quantum field theory, general relativity or non-Lorentzian theories.

Send: CV, transcript and a short statement of research interests.

  • Duration: about 18 months; extension depends on remaining project time and funding.
  • Funding: according to TÜBİTAK scholarship rules and the project budget. Eligible Ph.D. students and postdocs may also apply for the TÜBİTAK 2250 program. Applicants must meet TÜBİTAK eligibility rules.
  • Deadline: applications are reviewed until the positions are filled.
  • Undergraduates interested in these topics are also welcome to get in touch about research opportunities.

How to apply: email utku.zorba@iuc.edu.tr.
